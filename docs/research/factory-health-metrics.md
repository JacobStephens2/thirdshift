# Measuring autonomous software-factory health: actionable signals vs. vanity metrics

Research date: 2026-11-11, for issue #2036 (a **Metrics** view on the **Console** for understanding `thirdshift` factory health and improvement opportunities over time), building on Spec #981 (**Run metrics**), #1036 (`thirdshift metrics`), and #813 (Console charts).

**Question.** Which concrete metrics, ratios, and breakdowns genuinely help the **Day shift** operating an autonomous coding-agent software factory identify failing areas, wasted spend and time, regressions across releases, **Harnesses**, and **Models**, and high-leverage improvements — as opposed to vanity or noisy metrics ("metrics for metrics' sake")?

**Method.**

- Investigated three bodies of primary evidence, citing each claim to the source that owns it and marking anything not verified on a primary page as **[unverified]**:
  1. **Software-delivery and SRE measurement literature:** DORA / *Accelerate* and DORA's 2024–2025 5-metric and AI capabilities research; Forsgren, Storey et al.'s SPACE framework (*ACM Queue*, 2021); Noda, Storey, Forsgren & Greiler's DevEx framework (*ACM Queue*, 2023); and the Google SRE chapters on monitoring and SLOs.
  2. **2025–2026 coding-agent observability surfaces and trajectory studies:** Devin (Session Insights, ACU sizing, Enterprise Metrics API v3), GitHub Copilot coding agent (Mission Control, session tracking, Usage Metrics API), OpenAI Codex (review pane, automations inbox, Analytics API), Factory (Missions, OpenTelemetry Analytics API), Anthropic's empirical Claude Code autonomy study, Princeton's *AI Agents That Matter* and *Holistic Agent Leaderboard (HAL)*, and empirical failure taxonomies on SWE-bench, OpenHands, and multi-agent LLM trajectories.
  3. **`thirdshift` source code and recent Fleet retrospective issues:** [`src/logs/run_metrics.rs`](../../src/logs/run_metrics.rs), [`src/logs/session_signals.rs`](../../src/logs/session_signals.rs), [`src/logs/metrics/trends.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/trends.rs), [`src/logs/metrics.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics.rs), [`src/logs/command_record.rs`](../../src/logs/command_record.rs), [`src/logs/usual_range.rs`](../../src/logs/usual_range.rs), [`src/scoring.rs`](../../src/scoring.rs), [`src/logs/metrics/bug_origins.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/bug_origins.rs), [`src/logs/reading/inbox.rs`](../../src/logs/reading/inbox.rs), [`src/recovery.rs`](../../src/recovery.rs), [`src/failed_run.rs`](../../src/failed_run.rs), [`src/session/kind.rs`](../../src/session/kind.rs), and [`src/console.rs`](../../src/console.rs).
- Mapped every concept onto [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md) vocabulary (notably **Console** rather than *dashboard* or *web UI*, **Ticket run** rather than the legacy *Run* where naming the domain concept, **Session trace** rather than *Session log*, **Fleet** rather than *hosts*, and **Run metrics** kept apart rather than any *KPI* or *health score*).

**Out of scope.** Designing visual chart components or CSS tokens ([ADR 0018](../adr/0018-the-console-has-one-layout-and-themes.md)); inventing a cross-provider token price table (explicitly ruled out in #981); modifying code in this research step.

## TL;DR

- **Pair four dimensions in tension; never compute a single "health score".** Both SPACE ([Forsgren et al., 2021](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3454124)) and DORA's 5-metric model ([dora.dev](https://dora.dev/guides/)) show that single metrics fail under Goodhart's Law, and DORA's 2024–2025 AI research shows faster generation amplifies downstream instability and rework unless paired with quality gates ([DORA AI research](https://dora.dev/research/)). `thirdshift`'s glossary already codifies this ("Each is kept apart, never combined into one number"; _Avoid_: `health score`, `KPI`). A useful **Metrics** view pairs **(1) Outcome & PR merge yield**, **(2) Post-merge quality & Day shift intervention burden**, **(3) Cost, time & wasted-effort efficiency**, and **(4) Stage bottlenecks & session trajectory friction**.
- **Normalize cost and time by *succeeded delivery*, and separate wasted effort from clean runs.** Agent evaluation research ([Kapoor et al., arXiv:2407.01502](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01502); [HAL, arXiv:2510.11977](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977)) and Devin/GitHub enterprise metrics converge on **cost per resolved/merged PR** (including failed attempts) rather than cost per attempt. In [`src/logs/metrics/trends.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/trends.rs) (`:258-264`), `total_minutes`, `repairs`, and `ci_rounds` currently average across succeeded and failed runs together — meaning a spike in 30-second early crashes *lowers* mean duration and mean **Repairs**, making a broken week look faster and cleaner.
- **Distinguish first-pass clean deliveries from Repair-loop recoveries.** A **Ticket run** that lands with `repairs == 0` is qualitatively healthier than one rescued by three **Repairs** and a **Check re-run**. `RunMetrics` already records `repairs` and `ci_rounds` per run ([`src/logs/run_metrics.rs`](../../src/logs/run_metrics.rs) `:118-119`), but `trends::weekly` collapses `repairs` into a mean instead of exposing the **first-pass clean rate** (`succeeded && repairs == 0`).
- **Trajectory-friction counters (`repeated_tool_calls`, `tool_errors`, `permission_denials`, `killed_work`) diagnose *why* sessions degrade.** SWE-bench and OpenHands failure studies ([Liu et al., arXiv:2509.13941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13941); [Cemri et al., arXiv:2503.13657](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657)) find that failed trajectories are significantly longer and dominated by repetitive tool-call thrashing and environment/tool errors. `thirdshift` already extracts all four signals plus `destructive_commands` from every **Session trace** ([`src/logs/session_signals.rs`](../../src/logs/session_signals.rs) `:15-24`).
- **Avoid five noisy traps:** (1) pooling heterogeneous **Command** kinds (`Ticket run`, `Triage run`, `Architect run`, `Scoring run`) into one pass rate; (2) raw token sums without per-succeeded-PR normalization or **Model**/**Harness** splits (especially given #1639 and #1651); (3) treating raw `review_findings` counts as quality (#2051 showed declined observations inflating counts to 10–12 on small PRs); (4) counting **Pass** skips as failures; and (5) lines-of-code volume.
- **Small schema & reader gaps block root-cause attribution at scale:**
  1. `CommandRecord` lacks `thirdshift_version` (even though `Session trace` OTLP attributes already write `service.version` at [`src/session/trace.rs`](../../src/session/trace.rs) `:65`), hiding regressions introduced by `thirdshift update`.
  2. `CommandRecord::finish` discards `account.recovery` (`recovery::Stage` in [`src/recovery.rs`](../../src/recovery.rs) `:23-34`) and `FailedRun::held()` / `waiting()` ([`src/failed_run.rs`](../../src/failed_run.rs) `:30-38`), forcing the **Day shift** to read free-text `cause` or open **Command logs** to tell pre-worktree failures, `implement` crashes, **Inherited failures**, **Declined CI fixes**, and **Self-merge** holds apart.
  3. `trends::weekly` conflates `state == "died"` crashes with `state == "live"` Working Commands inside `missing_outcomes` ([`src/logs/metrics/trends.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/trends.rs) `:252-256`).
  4. `StageName::is_session` ([`src/logs/command_record.rs`](../../src/logs/command_record.rs) `:287-302`) omits `CodeReview`, `ProcessStep`, `SecurityCloseCheck`, and `Grading` even though `session::Logged::stage` ([`src/session/kind.rs`](../../src/session/kind.rs) `:134-159`) maps sessions onto them — so even after #2047 persists `RunMetrics` for **Review runs**, `trends::weekly` will silently drop `"Code review"` **Session trace** signals unless `is_session` is updated.

## 1. Software-delivery and engineering-systems measurement

### 1.1 DORA / *Accelerate* and the 2024–2025 Rework expansion

- **Throughput paired with Instability.** Forsgren, Humble & Kim's *Accelerate* (2018) and the DORA program established that software delivery cannot be judged by speed alone or stability alone; high-performing systems measure both together ([DORA guides](https://dora.dev/guides/)). DORA's updated **5-metric model** groups these into two factors ([DORA guides](https://dora.dev/guides/), [DORA research](https://dora.dev/research/)):
  - **Software Delivery Throughput:** *Deployment frequency*, *Change lead time*, and *Failed deployment recovery time* (moved from stability into throughput because fast-flowing pipelines also ship fixes quickly).
  - **Software Delivery Instability:** *Change fail rate* (the share of deployments requiring immediate remediation or rollback) and **Deployment rework rate** (the share of deployments that are unplanned fixes for user-facing bugs introduced earlier).
- **Why Rework Rate was added — and why it matters for AI factories.** DORA added *Deployment rework rate* because high deployment frequency can conceal reactive firefighting: a pipeline shipping ten PRs a day where four repair regressions caused by earlier PRs looks healthy on volume and lead time while losing 40% of capacity to defects ([DORA research](https://dora.dev/research/)).
- **AI as an amplifier and the verification tax.** Across DORA's 2024 and 2025 AI research cycles, higher AI adoption improved local code-generation speed and perceived individual flow, but showed a negative relationship with software delivery stability when automated testing, small batch sizes, and review guardrails lagged behind ([DORA AI research](https://dora.dev/research/)). Generating pull requests faster without measuring downstream bug introduction shifts the bottleneck into an unmeasured human "verification and rework tax."
  - *For `thirdshift`:* Throughput (merged or ready-for-review pull requests per week, and `total_minutes`) must always sit beside `confirmed_bug_origins` (unplanned post-merge bug-introducing PRs), calibrated **Scores**, and **Repair loop** churn.

### 1.2 SPACE and DevEx: metrics in tension and human cognitive load

- **SPACE's three anti-vanity rules.** Forsgren, Storey, Maddila, Zimmermann, Houck & Butler, *"The SPACE of Developer Productivity: There's more to it than you think"* (*ACM Queue* 19(1), 2021, [queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3454124](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3454124)), refutes five recurring measurement myths, three of which apply directly to an autonomous software factory:
  1. **Activity is not productivity (Myth 1):** counts of commits, lines of code, pull requests opened, or tokens burned measure motion, not delivered value.
  2. **No single metric or composite score works (Myth 3):** collapsing dimensions into one index obscures trade-offs and invites Goodhart's Law (optimizing the score at the expense of unmeasured quality or human burden).
  3. **Hold at least three dimensions in tension:** SPACE spans **S**atisfaction/well-being, **P**erformance (outcomes: reliability, absence of bugs), **A**ctivity, **C**ommunication/collaboration (review quality and hand-off clarity), and **E**fficiency/flow (uninterrupted progress, low friction).
- **DevEx's three friction drivers.** Noda, Storey, Forsgren & Greiler, *"DevEx: What Actually Drives Productivity"* (*ACM Queue* 21(2), 2023, [queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3595878](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3595878)), identifies **Feedback loops**, **Cognitive load**, and **Flow state** as the actionable system drivers behind engineering output:
  - In `thirdshift`, **Feedback loops** map directly to `CI watch` duration, **Abandoned job** / **Check re-run** delays, and **Code review** / **Repair** stage times.
  - **Cognitive load** and **Flow state** apply to the **Day shift**: every false-alarm **Needs you** item, every sprawling or broken `ready for review` pull request that needs `thirdshift fix`, and every uncalibrated **Score** interrupts human focus.

### 1.3 Google SRE: symptoms vs. causes, and order statistics over means

- **Separate symptoms from causes.** The Google SRE Book distinguishes *symptoms* ("what is broken for the user/operator") from *causes* ("which internal subsystem is degraded") ([SRE: Monitoring Distributed Systems](https://sre.google/sre-book/monitoring-distributed-systems/)). Top-level health views should lead with symptoms (failed **Commands**, unmerged pull requests, confirmed **Bug origins**, wasted spend) and let the operator drill one level into causes (stopping stage, **Harness**/**Model** version, CI vs. session failure).
- **Means lie on skewed distributions.** SRE's SLO guidance warns that arithmetic means hide tail regressions and bimodal behavior (for example, fast failures mixed with slow timeouts), recommending percentiles / interquartile bounds and threshold exceedance shares ([SRE: Service Level Objectives](https://sre.google/sre-book/service-level-objectives/)). `thirdshift`'s **Usual range** (p25–p75 over the repository's last 30 **Command records** in [`src/logs/usual_range.rs`](../../src/logs/usual_range.rs) `:43-70`) and `Position::Above` counts already follow this principle; `Signal.mean` in `trends.rs` alone does not.

### 2.2 Academic agent-evaluation and trajectory-failure taxonomies

- **Cost-per-resolved-task and the Pareto frontier.** Kapoor, Stroebl, Siegel, Nadgir & Narayanan (*"AI Agents That Matter"*, [arXiv:2407.01502](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01502)) and the *Holistic Agent Leaderboard (HAL)* (*"The Missing Infrastructure for AI Agent Evaluation"*, [arXiv:2510.11977](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977)) demonstrate two failure modes in agent benchmarking that apply directly to production factories:
  1. **Unbounded retry / token gaming:** accuracy (Pass@1 or PR merge rate) without cost/time bounds rewards brute-force scaffolding that burns 5–10× the tokens for marginal gains.
  2. **Cost-per-attempt illusion:** a cheaper **Model** or lower **Effort** that costs half as much per attempt but succeeds half as often (or triggers twice as many **Repairs** and human **Fix runs**) has *worse* **total cost per resolved issue**. Evaluating **Harnesses**, **Models**, and **Processes** requires pairing success rate with **total spend (succeeded + failed attempts) per succeeded delivery**.
- **Trajectory failure modes on SWE-bench and OpenHands.** Empirical analyses of issue-solving trajectories on SWE-bench ([Jimenez et al., arXiv:2310.06770](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770)) and OpenHands ([Wang et al., arXiv:2407.16741](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16741)) — notably Liu et al., *"An Empirical Study on Failures in Automated Issue Solving"* ([arXiv:2509.13941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13941)), and Cemri et al., *"Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?"* (MAST, [arXiv:2503.13657](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657)) — show that failed trajectories are systematically longer and higher-variance than succeeded ones, clustering into four observable trace patterns:
  1. **Repetitive tool-call / verification thrashing:** re-invoking identical read/search commands or repeating edit–test–fail cycles without changing strategy (directly measured by `repeated_tool_calls` and `repairs`).
  2. **Tool and environment execution errors:** malformed tool invocations, missing dependencies, sandbox/permission blocks, or broken base branches (measured by `tool_errors`, `permission_denials`, and **Inherited failures**).
  3. **Premature / incomplete termination and coordination drift:** stopping while background processes are still running, failing to verify specifications, or losing state across sub-agents (measured by `killed_work` / **Resume** frequency, `Spec` **Scores**, and **Spec run** Ticket blockers).
  4. **Late-stage regression / coherence collapse:** passing local edits then breaking unrelated checks or oscillating during CI repair until hitting retry caps (measured by `ci_rounds`, **Declined CI fixes**, and **Repair** budget exhaustion).

## 3. Cross-reference against `thirdshift` source code

### 3.1 Signals `thirdshift` already captures that diagnose real factory problems

`thirdshift` already captures rich, structured facts across four layers: [`CommandRecord`](../../src/logs/command_record.rs) (`:85-140`), [`RunMetrics`](../../src/logs/run_metrics.rs) (`:13-51`), [`SessionSignals`](../../src/logs/session_signals.rs) (`:15-40`), and [`scoring`](../../src/scoring.rs) / [`ConfirmedCounts`](../../src/logs/metrics/bug_origins.rs) (`:10-33`), plus Kept GitHub settlement facts in [`inbox.rs`](../../src/logs/reading/inbox.rs) (`:828-918`). Grouped into the four tension pillars, these already support high-leverage operational diagnosis:

#### Pillar A — Outcome & funnel yield (sliced by `Command` kind, never pooled)

- **Delivery success rate per kind** (from `CommandRecord.kind` and `CommandRecord.outcome`, [`src/logs/command_record.rs`](../../src/logs/command_record.rs) `:87, 108`):
  - For **Ticket runs** (`kind == "Run"`), **Spec runs** (`"Spec run"`), **Base fixes** (`"Base fix"`), and **Fix runs** (`"Fix run"`): share ending `merged` or `ready for review` vs. `failed`, `review required`, `interrupted`, or `state == "died"`, with `paused` kept separate (per [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md): "Paused Command … is neither success nor failure").
  - For **Triage runs** (`filed` / `recommended` vs. `failed`), **Architect runs** (`plan published` / `idea filed` / `idea already filed` vs. `review failed` or dispatched failure, [`src/run_ending.rs`](../../src/run_ending.rs) `:141-143`), **Security runs** (`findings recorded` / fix outcome vs. `pre-audit failed` / `audit failed` / `reproduction failed`, `:144-193`), and **Review runs**: each kind gets its own outcome breakdown.
- **Delivered-PR merge survival rate** (from `Inbox` Kept GitHub state, [`src/logs/reading/inbox.rs`](../../src/logs/reading/inbox.rs) `:122-125, 908-917`):
  - Among **Commands** that ended `ready for review`, the share whose pull request subsequently settled via `MERGED_REASON` (`"pull request merged"`) vs. `CLOSED_REASON` (`"pull request closed"` unmerged) or required a follow-up **Fix run** (`TAKEN_UP_REASON` or `fix_command` when `InboxCi::Red` / `InboxMergeable::Conflicting` at `:183-189`). This is GitHub's and Devin's exact antidote to "agent opened a PR nobody wanted to merge."
- **Crash (`died`) rate** ([`src/logs/reading.rs`](../../src/logs/reading.rs) `:414-420`):
  - Share of **Commands** with `outcome.is_none()` and `!record.live(...)` — immediately catches OOMs, machine reboots, or unhandled panics.

#### Pillar B — Post-merge quality & Day shift attention cost (the stability counterweight)

- **Confirmed Bug origin rate** ([`src/logs/metrics/bug_origins.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/bug_origins.rs) `:10-33`, [`src/scoring/origins.rs`](../../src/scoring/origins.rs)):
  - Confirmed **Bug origins** per week and per `(repository, harness, model, effort)` divided by merged **Ticket runs** / **Spec runs** in that cohort. Because unconfirmed and rejected proposals are excluded (`read_confirmed_origins`), every count is a human-verified post-merge defect — `thirdshift`'s exact counterpart to DORA's *Change Fail Rate* and *Deployment Rework Rate*.
- **Weekly Scoring pass rates with calibration gating** ([`src/scoring.rs`](../../src/scoring.rs) `:186-209`, [`src/scoring/grader.rs`](../../src/scoring/grader.rs) `:16-42`, [`src/scoring/calibration.rs`](../../src/scoring/calibration.rs) `:79-118`):
  - Pass rates across `Correct`, `Scoped`, `Tested`, `Honest`, and `Standards` on sampled merged pull requests, explicitly badged with `State.calibrated` (`compared == 20 && matches >= 18`) so **Grader** drift is never mistaken for factory regression.
- **Day shift intervention & Needs-you rate** ([`src/logs/reading/inbox.rs`](../../src/logs/reading/inbox.rs) `:86-94, 223-244`):
  - Share of **Commands** requiring human intervention (`Failed`, `Died`, `Triage` after a manual **Stop** without `--requeue`, `Paused` at plan limits, `Finding` waiting on **Day shift** triage, and live `Silent` / `Slow` episodes).
- **Repository Failure-limit blockage** ([`src/logs/reading.rs`](../../src/logs/reading.rs) `:325-344`, [`src/logs/pass_starts.rs`](../../src/logs/pass_starts.rs)):
  - Whether a repository's **Pickup run** or **Triage run** **Pass** is currently held at its `agent-failed` **Failure limit** (or stale `last_fired_at`), exposing throughput starvation before the **Day shift** wonders why nothing ran overnight.

#### Pillar C — Efficiency, wasted effort & Repair-loop churn

- **First-pass clean rate vs. repaired success rate** ([`src/logs/run_metrics.rs`](../../src/logs/run_metrics.rs) `:14-16, 118-119`):
  - Among succeeded **Ticket runs** and **Spec runs**, the share with `repairs == 0` (clean first pass) vs. `repairs >= 1` (salvaged by the **Repair loop**). A falling first-pass clean rate is an early warning that initial `implement` / `Code review` quality or **Base branch** CI stability is degrading, even while final merge rate still holds steady.
- **Wasted-time and wasted-cost share** (`RunMetrics.total_minutes` + `Signals.cost_usd` / `output_tokens` split by `outcome`):
  - Share of machine wall-clock minutes and reported `cost_usd` (or model tokens) consumed by `failed`, `died`, and `interrupted` **Commands** vs. succeeded deliveries.
- **Effective cost and wall-clock minutes per succeeded delivery**:
  - Total `cost_usd` (where reported by the **Harness**) and total session minutes across *all* attempts in a cohort divided by succeeded deliveries in that cohort — implementing the [HAL Pareto efficiency metric](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977).
- **Cache-read share by `(harness, model)`** ([`src/logs/session_signals.rs`](../../src/logs/session_signals.rs) `:27-33`):
  - `cache_read_tokens / input_tokens` (valid across **Harnesses** because [`src/session/usage.rs`](../../src/session/usage.rs) normalizes `input_tokens` to include cached tokens). A sudden drop after a **Harness** version bump (`harness_version`) or prompt edit immediately diagnoses context-cache invalidation.

#### Pillar D — Stage bottlenecks & session trajectory friction

- **Stage bottleneck exceedance (`Position::Above`) alongside `UsualRange`** ([`src/logs/run_metrics.rs`](../../src/logs/run_metrics.rs) `:26-51`, [`src/logs/usual_range.rs`](../../src/logs/usual_range.rs) `:30-74`):
  - Per `(repository, stage)`, the share of completed stages landing `Position::Above` the repository's p25–p75 **Usual range** (from `trends::Stage.positions`, [`src/logs/metrics/trends.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/trends.rs) `:152, 177`). Separates agent slowdowns (`implement`, `Code review`, `Spec review`, `Repair`) from external GitHub Actions runner/test slowdowns (`CI watch`).
- **Trajectory friction rates per session** ([`src/logs/session_signals.rs`](../../src/logs/session_signals.rs) `:18-24, 201-244`):
  - `repeated_tool_calls` (exact `(tool, input)` duplicates within one session, `:228-230`): catches search/polling thrashing (such as #2050's repeated `gh pr view` polling).
  - `tool_errors` (`Outcome::Failed`, `:232`): catches **Model** or **Harness** tool-schema/shell regressions when sliced by `(harness, harness_version, model)`.
  - `permission_denials` (`Outcome::Denied`, `:233`): under [ADR 0013](../adr/0013-every-harness-runs-unattended-and-fully-trusted.md), every **Harness** runs unattended and trusted; *any* non-zero `permission_denials` flags a broken CLI flag, hook, or sandbox regression.
  - `killed_work` (`:191-215, 243`) and `Resume` stage count: measures how often sessions leave background tasks running at turn end and require a **Resume**.
  - `destructive_commands` (`force-push`, `shared-history-rewrite`, `recursive-delete-outside-worktree` in [`src/logs/session_signals/destructive.rs`](../../src/logs/session_signals/destructive.rs) `:27-121`): an advisory safety counter where *any* match > 0 warrants direct inspection rather than weekly averaging.

### 3.2 Tempting metrics that are noisy or misleading ("metrics for metrics' sake")

| Tempting metric | Why it misleads or creates noise in `thirdshift` | Source / code evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Composite "Factory Health Score" (0–100)** | Hides opposing regressions (e.g., faster **Ticket runs** cancelling out rising **Bug origins**) and invites arbitrary weights. Explicitly forbidden by domain rules. | [SPACE Myth #3](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3454124); [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md) (`Run metrics`, _Avoid_: `health score`, `KPI`); Spec #981 Q1.1. |
| **2. ### 3.3 Concrete gaps in `CommandRecord`, `RunMetrics`, and `trends.rs`

Inspecting the recording and aggregation path reveals six small, concrete gaps where `thirdshift` already computes or observes a high-leverage fact during execution, then either drops it before persistence or blurs it during weekly aggregation:

1. **`trends::weekly` conflates `died` crashes with `live` runs and pools failed with succeeded metrics** ([`src/logs/metrics/trends.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/trends.rs) `:251-264`):
   - `Snapshot` already computes `command.state` as `"ended"`, `"live"`, or `"died"` ([`src/logs/reading.rs`](../../src/logs/reading.rs) `:414-420`), but `trends::collect` branches only on `if let Some(outcome) = &record.outcome`, dumping both `"live"` Working Commands and `"died"` crashed Commands into `missing_outcomes` (`:255`).
   - In the same block (`:258-264`), `repairs`, `ci_rounds`, and `total_minutes` are added to one `Signal` regardless of outcome, and `first_pass_clean` (`repairs == 0` among succeeded deliveries) is not tallied.
   - *High-leverage unlock:* split `died` vs. `live`, count `first_pass_clean` among succeeded deliveries, and split `total_minutes` / `cost_usd` into succeeded vs. non-succeeded buckets (or exclude `live` Commands from completed outcome/efficiency denominators).

2. **Failed runs discard structured stopping stage and failure classification at completion** ([`src/logs/command_record.rs`](../../src/logs/command_record.rs) `:359-384`, [`src/recovery.rs`](../../src/recovery.rs) `:23-34`, [`src/failed_run.rs`](../../src/failed_run.rs) `:27-70`):
   - While ending a failed run, `Account<'_>` holds `account.recovery` (containing `recovery::Stage`: `Implementation`, `Tickets`, `SpecReview`, `SecurityReview`, `Push`, `PullRequest`, `Repair`, `Ci`, `SelfMerge`, `BaseFix`, plus `Preservation`), `account.interrupted`, `FailedRun::held()` (`PolicyRefusal`, `security::review::Hold`, `MergeHold`), and `FailedRun::waiting()` (a **Spec run** whose undone **Tickets** are all only waiting).
   - `CommandRecord::finish` (`:366-376`) discards those structured fields and writes only `outcome` and free-text `cause: Option<String>`. Today, answering *"Did this week's 18 failures happen in pre-worktree acquisition, `implement` session crashes, **Inherited failures**, **Declined CI fixes**, or **Self-merge** policy holds?"* requires regex-matching `cause` strings or opening 18 **Command logs**.
   - *High-leverage unlock:* persist an optional structured `stopped_at` stage (falling back for historical records to `stages.last().map(|s| s.name)` or `"pre-stage"` when `stages.is_empty()`) and a small failure-kind enum derived deterministically from `FailedRun` / `Account` (never from agent prose, matching `Recovery`'s own rule).

3. **`CommandRecord` records schema `version: 2`, not `thirdshift_version`** ([`src/logs/command_record.rs`](../../src/logs/command_record.rs) `:86, 427`):
   - `env!("CARGO_PKG_VERSION")` is already stamped on **Session trace** OTLP resources as `service.version` ([`src/session/trace.rs`](../../src/session/trace.rs) `:65`) and on **Experiment** verdicts ([`src/verdict.rs`](../../src/verdict.rs) `:197`), and `CommandRecord` already flattens `process: Identity` with `process_name`, `process_kind`, and `process_hash` ([`src/processes/identity.rs`](../../src/processes/identity.rs) `:7-26`).
   - However, `CommandRecord` has no `thirdshift_version` field, [`src/logs/session_signals.rs`](../../src/logs/session_signals.rs) (`:139-145`) reads `thirdshift.harness.version` while ignoring `service.version`, and `trends::Group` ([`src/logs/metrics/trends.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/trends.rs) `:17-26`) cannot group or filter by `thirdshift` release.
   - *High-leverage unlock:* write `thirdshift_version` on new `CommandRecord`s (and optionally read `service.version` from retained **Session traces** for older records) so regressions after `thirdshift update` separate cleanly from **Harness** (`harness_version`) or **Model** changes.

4. **`CommandRecord::finish` omits `Review run`, `Triage run`, `Fix run`, and `Process` — and `StageName::is_session` omits `Code review` and `Process Step`** ([`src/logs/command_record.rs`](../../src/logs/command_record.rs) `:287-302, 376`):
   - Issue #2047 (from the 2026-11-11 **Fleet** retrospective: 0/245 **Review run**, **Triage run**, and **Fix run** records across 6 **Fleet** machines persisted `run_metrics`) identified the `matches!(self.kind.as_str(), "Run" | "Spec run" | "Base fix")` guard at `command_record.rs:376`, and #1655 identified `Work::Process` bypassing `finish` via `logs::complete`.
   - **Additional source finding for #2036 / #2047:** [`src/session/kind.rs`](../../src/session/kind.rs) (`:134-159`) maps `Kind::CodeReview` to `Stage::CodeReview` (`"Code review"`), `Kind::ProcessStep(_)` to `Stage::ProcessStep` (`"Process Step"`), `Kind::SecurityCloseCheck` to `Stage::SecurityCloseCheck` (`"Security close check"`), and `Kind::ScoringGrader(_)` to `Stage::Grading` (`"grading"`). None of those four variants is listed in `StageName::is_session` ([`src/logs/command_record.rs`](../../src/logs/command_record.rs) `:287-302`). Because `trends::weekly` only aggregates `session_signals` when `StageName::is_session(&stage.name)` is true ([`src/logs/metrics/trends.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/trends.rs) `:288-334`), **Review run** `"Code review"` sessions (and `"Security close check"` / `"Process Step"` sessions) are routed to the non-session branch and their tokens, costs, and tool errors are silently omitted from weekly trends!

5. **Run-level vs. session-level token/cost rollup in `trends::weekly`** ([`src/logs/metrics/trends.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics/trends.rs) `:286-336`):
   - In `trends::weekly`, `row.session_signals` is populated *only* on session rows (`session_kind: Some(...)`), while Run-level rows (`session_kind: None`) have empty `session_signals`. That avoids double-counting when summing across rows, and properly attributes a **Security review** running on a different `security.harness` ([ADR 0021](../adr/0021-a-command-may-run-its-security-review-on-another-harness.md)). However, it means a reader looking at Run-level rows has no Run-total `cost_usd` or `tool_errors` to pair directly with `outcomes` for "cost per succeeded **Ticket run**" without re-joining session rows that may have different `(harness, model, effort)` keys.

6. **Account and Fleet scope + host-suspension caveats** ([`src/logs/metrics.rs`](../../src/logs/metrics.rs) `:104-120`, [`src/console.rs`](../../src/console.rs) `:557-564, 1455-1487`):
   - `/metrics` already exists in `src/console.rs` as the route twin of `thirdshift metrics --json`, but currently renders `<pre>{escaped json}</pre>` (`:1480`) and is not linked in [`src/console/nav.html`](../../src/console/nav.html).
   - `metrics::read` calls `Snapshot::load_metrics(&config.logs_dir, ...)` (`src/logs/metrics.rs:105`), which reads only the serving OS account's `logs_dir` — unlike `Snapshot::for_user` ([`src/logs/reading.rs`](../../src/logs/reading.rs) `:50-110`), which merges opted-in **Other-account Commands**. Nor does `/metrics` currently aggregate across paired **Fleet** machines (which under [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-each-console-serves-an-api-that-can-do-what-the-cli-can.md) must happen as an explicit client read of each owning machine's `/api/v1/metrics`, just as `cronjobs --fleet` does, rather than relaying tokens inside the API).
   - Known upstream measurement limits that the UI should label rather than hide: `Signal.missing` vs. `Signal.observed` for older records (#981), host **Suspended time** inflating wall-clock stages until #2026/#2034 lands, missing Codex native sub-agent usage (#1651), and uncounted Bash-spawned child CLIs (#1639).

## 4. Synthesis: what belongs on the Console Metrics view

To satisfy [ADR 0016](../adr/0016-the-console-is-a-long-running-reader.md), [ADR 0018](../adr/0018-the-console-has-one-layout-and-themes.md), [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-each-console-serves-an-api-that-can-do-what-the-cli-can.md), and Spec #981's rule that every signal keeps its own units with missing values explicit, a high-signal `/metrics` page needs four focused views over the same weekly/cohort JSON, filterable by time window (`--from` / `--through`), `repository`, `kind`, and setup (`harness`, `model`, `effort`, `thirdshift_version`):

1. **Outcomes & Failure Breakdown (by `Command` kind, week over week):**
   - Ended count, succeeded share (`merged` + `ready for review` for delivery kinds; kind-specific outcomes for **Triage run**, **Architect run**, **Security run**, **Review run**), **first-pass clean share** (`succeeded` with `repairs == 0`), `failed`, `died`, `interrupted`, and `paused`.
   - Adjacent **Where failures stopped** breakdown (pre-stage/acquisition, `implement`, `Code review`, `Security review`, `CI watch` — split into **Inherited failure** vs. **Declined CI fix** vs. branch CI — `Repair` cap, `Self-merge` hold) so the **Day shift** sees the dominant failure mode at a glance, with links to filtered **History** rows.
2. **Efficiency, Cost & Waste (succeeded vs. wasted split):**
   - Succeeded delivery median/IQR `total_minutes` vs. **wasted minutes** spent on failed/died/interrupted **Commands**.
   - Reported `cost_usd` (where available) and `tokens_by_model` (`input`, `output`, `cache_read / input` hit rate), showing **effective cost per succeeded PR** (all attempts' spend ÷ succeeded PRs) alongside **wasted spend share**, with explicit `observed / missing` coverage counts so `cost_usd: null` on Codex is never read as `$0.00`.
3. **Stage Bottlenecks & Session Friction (by repository and setup):**
   - Per `(repository, stage)`: **Usual range** (p25–p75 minutes) and `% Position::Above`, isolating whether slowdowns sit in `CI watch` or in agent sessions.
   - Per `(session_kind, harness, harness_version, model, effort)`: `tool_errors` per session, `repeated_tool_calls` per session, `killed_work` / `Resume` rate, `permission_denials` (highlighted whenever > 0), and `destructive_commands` flags (highlighted whenever > 0, linking to the **Command**).
4. **Post-merge Quality & Day shift Burden (cross-linked to `/scores` and `/bug-origins`):**
   - Confirmed **Bug origins** attributed to runs in each week/setup cohort (`confirmed_bug_origins`), plus a compact summary or link to `/scores` criterion pass rates (`Correct`, `Scoped`, `Tested`, `Honest`, `Standards` with calibration status) and PR merge survival / **Needs you** intervention rates.

### Key questions for the `#2036` grilling session

1. **Scope of machines and accounts:** Should `/metrics` show only the serving machine (matching `thirdshift metrics` today), include opted-in **Other-account Commands** on the same machine (matching `status` and the **Inbox**), and/or offer a `--fleet` client aggregation across paired **Fleet** machines (matching `cronjobs --fleet` under ADR 0026)?
2. **CLI parity boundary (`thirdshift metrics --json`):** Should we enrich `thirdshift metrics --json` directly with outcome-split efficiency signals (`first_pass_clean`, `died` vs. `live`, succeeded vs. failed minutes/spend) and stopping-stage/failure-kind breakdowns so `/metrics` remains a pure projection of `thirdshift metrics --json`?
3. **Schema additions vs. read-time derivation:** Should #2036 (or prerequisite Tickets alongside #2047) add `thirdshift_version` and structured failure `stopped_at` / failure category to `CommandRecord`, while deriving fallback values (`stages.last()`, OTLP `service.version`) for existing historical records?
4. **Relationship to `/scores` and `/bug-origins`:** Confirmed **Bug origins** are already joined into `thirdshift metrics`, whereas weekly **Scores** live in `thirdshift scores` (which covers sampled PRs across all **Fleet** machines). Should `/metrics` stay scoped to `thirdshift metrics` (with a link to `/scores`), or display calibrated criterion pass rates alongside run friction?
5. **Visual form on desktop and phone:** Tables + text ratios + compact CSS/SVG sparklines or stacked bars (resolving #813's open question), or strictly tabular numbers first?
