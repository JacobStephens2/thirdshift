Efficiency, wasted effort and Repair churn

Minutes, Repairs and CI rounds are means over Succeeded Commands only, so fast crashes cannot make them look better. First-pass clean is Succeeded with no Repair. Per success divides every ended attempt's minutes or spend by the Succeeded count. Wasted is the Failed (died included) and Stopped share of that time or spend. Observed and missing count the attempts that did or did not record it.
